# Bundled theme fonts

- **Bergoom** (Regular, Semibold, Bold) is by Dmitry Chestnykh and is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/dchest/bergoom
- **Fixedsys Excelsior 3.02** is maintained by Kirill Pertsev. Its creators released it to the public domain, with CC0 as the fallback dedication where needed. Source: https://github.com/kika/fixedsys

Benne and DotGothic16 are loaded from Google Fonts at runtime and are not bundled in this folder.
